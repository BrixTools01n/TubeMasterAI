Privacy Policy for TubeMaster AI

Transparent information about how TubeMaster AI collects, uses, protects, and deletes your personal and account data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: August 19, 2026 Application: TubeMaster AI (Android App & Connected Account Deletion Services)

1. Introduction

Welcome to TubeMaster AI ("we", "our", or "the App"). TubeMaster AI is a creator tools application offering 100 dedicated AI creation tools for YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy rights. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, handling, retention, and deletion practices across our Android application and our external account management services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and handle information strictly necessary to provide creator tools and manage account security:

  • Account & Authentication: When you sign in via Google Sign-In or email, we process your email address, display name, and unique user identifier to establish your account session and manage your subscription plan.
  • User Prompts & AI Generations: Topic inputs, keywords, and requested scripts processed via secure AI model APIs.
  • Local On-Device Data: Saved drafts in your tool vault, generation history logs, and local quota timestamps stored on your device.
  • Subscription & Payment References: Upgrade plan references and verification identifiers for Pro tier activation.

3. How Information Is Used

We use your data for legitimate creator workflows:

  • To generate video ideas, script outlines, and SEO recommendations.
  • To manage your account identity, plan status, and feature quotas.
  • To maintain local draft history and favorite creations.
  • To prevent automated abuse, spam, and brute-force activity.

We do not sell your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

4. AI & Third-Party Processing

TubeMaster AI integrates trusted AI APIs (such as Google Gemini API / Google Cloud services) to provide creator suggestions.

  • Prompts submitted for content generation are transmitted securely over TLS/HTTPS encrypted connections.
  • We do not sell user prompts or use personal creative drafts to train public foundation models without explicit consent.

5. Data Retention Policy

We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • Active Account Data: Maintained while your account remains active.
  • Statutory Financial Retention: Where required by applicable taxation or financial accounting laws, minimal billing/payment transaction reference identifiers are retained for the legally mandated period. All other personal profile records and prompt histories are purged upon deletion.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have the right to permanently delete your TubeMaster AI account and all associated personal data at any time through two official pathways:

1. In-App Deletion: Open TubeMaster AI > Profile Screen > Select "Remove Your Account" > Confirm. This immediately purges your local user profile, all generation histories, saved vault items, and active session tokens.

2. External Web Deletion Portal: Visit Account Deletion Portal. Sign in with the Google Account you use with TubeMaster AI, verify your profile identity, and confirm deletion.

Upon verified account deletion:

  • User Profile: Registered email, user ID, and credentials are permanently purged.
  • Generations & History: All AI prompt outputs, draft scripts, and history records are permanently deleted.
  • Saved Items: Tool vault drafts and saved templates are removed.
  • Session Invalidation: Active login sessions and credentials are immediately revoked.

7. Security Safeguards

We maintain robust security controls including cryptographic hashing of one-time verification tokens, TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit, strict Content Security Policy, X-Frame-Options protection, and automated IP rate limiting to prevent unauthorized account access or deletion abuse.

8. Children's Privacy

TubeMaster AI is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
