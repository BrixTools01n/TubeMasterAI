How the Deletion Process Works

To prevent unauthorized account deletion, our system sends a short-lived, single-use 6-digit verification code to the registered email address.

  • Step 1: Enter your registered account email and reason.
  • Step 2: Enter the verification code dispatched to your email.
  • Step 3: Once verified, account data is permanently purged.
2

What data is deleted?

When deletion is confirmed, all data associated with your TubeMaster AI account is permanently deleted:

  • Account & Profile: Registered email address, display name, and avatar.
  • Authentication Records: OAuth tokens, credential mappings, and login sessions.
  • Generated Content: Saved titles, descriptions, tags, and script drafts.
  • App History: Generation logs, quota tracking records, and user preferences.
  • Other Personal Data: Any custom configurations saved to your user ID.
3

Data retention

Certain limited records may be retained temporarily where strictly required by law, security protocols, or financial reporting:

  • Fraud Prevention & Abuse: Security logs to prevent repeat spam or platform abuse.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Transaction references or billing history as required by applicable tax/accounting laws.
  • Limited Scope: Retained data is strictly isolated, safeguarded, and purged automatically once retention obligations expire.
